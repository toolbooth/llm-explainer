# Standards crosswalk — all six planned modules

*Inside the Machine: Classroom Edition · shared front matter · draft 2026-08-22.*

**How to read this page.** Standards get renumbered: the CSTA 2026 identifiers are weeks old, AI4K12 is revising Big Idea 4, and Idaho and Utah are writing grade-band AI standards for 2027–28 [PRODUCT.md §9 "Standards churn"]. Every row therefore carries a **Verified against** date — the date on which the mapping was last checked against the named source document — and the table is re-verified each summer. Some mapping will be stale at any given moment; if a code on this page no longer matches the current published standard, the standard wins, and we would be grateful for an issue report.

**Status column.** M1 and M2 are the **MVP** (shipping first). M3–M6 are **planned**; their mappings are recorded now so that a teacher sequencing a unit can see where the series is going, but their pages do not yet exist [PRODUCT.md §10.1].

## Notes on the mapping

1. **Every module ends with an evaluation act** (the student judges the model's output), which is why HS-ALG-PS-05 and ISTE 1.3 apply, weakly, to every module even where the table names them only where they are central [PRODUCT.md §4.1 rule 5].
2. **The priorities report's own proposal.** The CSTA/AI4K12 priorities workshop proposed building "a small language model" from a children's book so students "understand conceptually how a large language model works – and why it sometimes delivers inaccurate output," and named Big Idea 4 as the one most in need of a generative-AI update [PRODUCT.md §1.2, §2.3; csteachers.org AI-Priorities-for-All-K-12-Students PDF]. M1–M4 on a model trained on children's stories is that proposal.
3. **AP CSP.** The CED has had no major change since Fall 2023. Unit 5 (Impact of Computing, 21–26 % of the exam) pairs with M4 + M6 [PRODUCT.md §4.4].
4. **Codes listed in PRODUCT.md §2.3 but mapped to no module:** HS-ALG-IM-10. It is recorded here so it is not lost; a future module or a block extension should claim it or it should be dropped from §2.3.
5. **Code used in §4.2 but absent from §2.3:** AP CSP CRD-1.A.4 (M6). Kept in the table; add it to the source list when §2.3 is next revised.
6. **"Author's extension"** marks a cell the author added beyond PRODUCT.md §4.2 because the activity plainly exercises the idea; treat these as proposals until verified.
7. **State standards.** Only California has a statutory *definition* (§33548) specific enough to map by element; it is the last content column. Other state items in PRODUCT.md §2.2 are policies or mandates, not standards with codes:
   - **Idaho SB 1227** (effective July 1, 2026) creates statewide grade-band "generative AI literacy standards" — codes not yet published; re-verify summer 2027.
   - **Utah HB 273 (2026)** expands CS standards to "AI awareness and ethical interaction; AI's role in information filtering and decision-making... critical evaluation of digital sources" — maps broadly to M4, M5, M6; codes pending.
   - **Alabama HB 329, Georgia SB 179, Mississippi SB 2294 (2026)** require a CS course "that includes AI instruction" for graduation (Alabama: class of 2032) — any module satisfies "includes AI instruction"; no codes.
   - **Ohio HB 96 / ORC 3301.24** is a district AI-policy mandate, not a content standard.
   - **Texas:** no TEKS update yet; the TACC/UT Austin task force (June 17, 2026) recommends a phased TEKS update.
   - **New York:** no NYSED standard; A9190 (pending) would restrict classroom AI before ninth grade — this series targets grades 9+.
8. **DOL TEN 07-25** tags are for community-college CTE instructors who cite the framework; the three areas used are *Understanding AI Principles* (M1–M3), *Evaluating AI Outputs* (M4–M5), *Using AI Responsibly* (M6). The framework's *Directing AI Effectively* and *Exploring AI Uses* areas are intentionally not claimed: this series does not teach prompting or use cases.
